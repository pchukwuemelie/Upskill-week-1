About Lion

The lion is the most social of all wild felid species, living in groups of related individuals with their offspring. Such a group is called a "pride". Groups of male lions are called "coalitions"

    • Lions spend much of their time resting; they are inactive for about twenty hours per day.
      Lions are apex and keystone predators, although they scavenge when opportunities occur.
      Male Asiatic lions are solitary or associate with up to three males, forming a loose pride while females associate with up to 12 other females, forming a stronger pride together with their cubs.
